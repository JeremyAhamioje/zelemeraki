The studio

A full-service design & build studio celebrating Nigeria's heritage through art and design.

Zelemeraki Design began as a minimalist interior and art studio. Today we are a complete construction company — architects, builders, landscapers and stylists working as one team, so your vision never gets lost between the drawing board and the site.

We curate spaces that exude an unrivalled air of serenity and simplicity, focusing on providing you with peace and relaxation of mind and body.

  • SimpleOnly what the space needs.
  • CleanHonest materials, quiet lines.
  • RootedLocal craft, Nigerian heritage.

— Founder, Zelemeraki Design

What we do

One studio, every stage of your build.

  1. Concept design, space planning, planning approvals and full construction drawings for residential, commercial and retail buildings.

  2. New builds delivered end-to-end — site management, trusted trades, procurement and quality control, with one point of contact throughout.

  3. We specialise in redesigning and revamping tired, old spaces, giving them a fresh new look — whether it's one living room or a whole building.

  4. Full project consultation, 3D rendering, material selection and furniture sourcing — plus décor and e-styling guidance for clients who'd like to redecorate themselves.

  5. Facades, cladding, lighting and outdoor living — so the outside of your home is as considered as the inside.

  6. Gardens, courtyards, pools and hardscaping that soften the architecture and bring calm to every approach.

“A home should not shout. It should exhale — and let you do the same.”

How we work

From first sketch to final reveal.

  1. I

    Consult

    We listen. A site visit or virtual call to understand how you live, work and what you want to feel in the space.

  2. II

    Concept & 3D

    Space planning, mood boards and photoreal 3D renders, with a clear budget and timeline before anything is built.

  3. III

    Build

    Our team manages construction, trades and procurement on site, with regular progress updates you can see.

  4. IV

    Style & Reveal

    Furniture, art and the final styling touches — then we hand you the keys to a space that's ready to breathe in.
