Many of our clients are building or renovating a home in Nigeria while living somewhere else entirely — London, Houston, Toronto, Dubai. Managing a project from a different time zone is hard, and it's where a lot of horror stories come from. It doesn't have to be.

Design happens on screen

We run the whole design stage remotely. It starts with a video consultation about how you live, what you love and what you want the space to feel like. From there you receive a mood board, a space plan and photoreal 3D renders, so you can walk through your future home before a single material is ordered. Changes happen on screen, where they're cheap, rather than on site, where they're not.

One team on the ground

The biggest risk for diaspora clients is fragmentation — a relative checking on a contractor, who's checking on a supplier. We manage construction, trades and procurement ourselves, so there's one team accountable for the result and one person you call.

You should be able to see your build progress from anywhere — without having to take anyone's word for it.

Progress you can actually see

Regular photo and video updates, clear milestones and itemised costs mean you always know where the project stands. Payments are tied to visible progress, not promises.

E-styling, if you'd rather do it yourself

Not every project needs a full build. If you have a space you'd like to refresh yourself, our e-styling service gives you a tailored design, a shopping list and layout guidance you can follow at your own pace — whether you're in the same city or on the other side of the world.

Where to start

Book a virtual consultation. We'll talk through the site, the budget and the timeline, and you'll come away with a clear picture of what building at home from abroad will involve.
